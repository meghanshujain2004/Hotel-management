Executive Overview &amp; System Architecture HotelCRM is an enterprise-grade sales acceleration, lead management, automated WhatsApp customer engagement, and SLA governance CRM built specifically for hotel sales and reservation teams. It unifies multi-channel inquiry capture (Instagram Lead Ads, WhatsApp Business, Facebook Ads, Website, and Manual Inquiries), provides customer support staff with an automated one-by-one calling queue, instantly triggers outcome-based WhatsApp message templates to guests after ca lls, and enforces a rigorous 2-tier escalation hierarchy. Role Primary Scope System Governance &amp; Permissions 👑 Admin (Hotel Owner / GM) Complete System Control Full hotel visibility, staff breach leaderboards, Level 2 critical escalation alerts, WhatsApp template builder, settings, and authority to reassign leads to any Manager. 👔 Manager (Sales Supervisor) Team &amp; Pipeline Oversight Oversees all hotel leads, team conversion leaderboards, Level 1 support escalation queue, and authority to reassign overdue leads to any Customer Support agent. 🎧 Customer Support Staff Frontline Calling &amp; Sales One-by-one sequential lead queue delivery, 1-tap call &amp; WhatsApp triggers, post-call disposition logging with automated guest WhatsApp template dispatch, and personal call history tracking. 2.
